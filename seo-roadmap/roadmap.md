## 1. Executive Summary

Alavanca has a structural SEO advantage most Calgary martial arts academies can't match: **three physical locations** spread across NE Calgary (HQ / Bridgeland), SW Calgary (Shawnessy), and Airdrie. Their current site uses only a fraction of this footprint — surfacing three `/locations/` pages and a handful of generic `/services/` pages.

The play for the next 6 months: build a **local SEO moat** using their three physical anchors to claim neighborhood-level search terms around Calgary and surrounding towns. Stack persona-specific pages (kids, women, over-40, beginners) and a glossary hub to catch top-of-funnel traffic before competitors do.

**Target outcome at Month 6:** 155 indexed pages · top-3 rankings for 40+ `{service} in {neighborhood}` terms · measurable lift in free-trial lead form submissions from organic traffic.

**Playbook mix (weighted for local intent):**
- **Locations** — 85 pages (heavy — core local SEO volume)
- **Personas** — 22 pages (heavy — kids, women, 40+, 50+, competitors)
- **Glossary** — 25 pages (top of funnel, cheap to rank on `alavancayyc.com`)
- **Curation / Best-in-City** — 8 pages (wins "best X near me" searches)
- **Comparisons** — 7 pages (BJJ vs X, Muay Thai vs X)
- **Pricing / Offers** — 8 pages (bottom-of-funnel capture)

## 3. URL Structure

New pages live under new top-level directories so they're organized and don't collide with the existing 15 pages:

```
/locations/{city}/{neighborhood}/{service}.html
    e.g. /locations/calgary/bridgeland/brazilian-jiu-jitsu.html
         /locations/calgary/shawnessy/kids-bjj.html
         /locations/airdrie/kingsview/muay-thai.html
         /locations/cochrane/bjj.html          (nearby-town single-slug)

/programs/{service}-for-{persona}.html
    e.g. /programs/bjj-for-adults-over-40.html
         /programs/kids-martial-arts-for-beginners.html

/guides/best-{service}-in-{city}.html
    e.g. /guides/best-bjj-gym-in-calgary.html
         /guides/best-kids-martial-arts-in-calgary.html

/learn/{term-slug}.html
    e.g. /learn/what-is-bjj.html
         /learn/bjj-belt-ranks.html

/compare/{term-slug}.html
    e.g. /compare/bjj-vs-wrestling.html
         /compare/gi-vs-no-gi-bjj.html

/memberships/{offer-slug}.html
    e.g. /memberships/family-pricing.html
         /memberships/drop-in-pricing.html
```

**Canonical rule:** never duplicate a neighborhood across two URLs. Each neighborhood gets exactly one canonical service variant — we pick the strongest-intent service per neighborhood (usually BJJ, sometimes kids BJJ or MMA).

| Phase | Month | Theme | Pages | Mix |

| 1 | Month 1 | Foundation — core NE + SW Calgary locations | 25 | 20 location pages (core neighborhoods near HQ + Shawnessy) + 5 foundational glossary |

| **Total** | | | **155** | |

**Rationale for ordering:**
1. **Month 1** targets highest-intent, closest-to-anchor neighborhoods first — pages most likely to rank quickly on fresh domain authority.
2. **Month 2** adds persona depth while extending the location footprint — this is where we lock in "kids BJJ Calgary" and "BJJ for women" type terms.
3. **Month 3** goes after the "best" queries because by now we have enough location coverage to legitimately compete on curation intent. Comparison pages cement topical authority.
4. **Month 4** fills out the glossary cluster, which amplifies the authority of the whole site (internal links from glossary to service/location pages).
5. **Month 5** captures commercial/pricing intent and expands north into Airdrie's full suburb list.
6. **Month 6** closes gaps and runs optimization passes on Phase 1 pages now that we know what's ranking.

## 5. Internal Linking Architecture (Hub & Spoke)

**Hubs (already exist on the site):**
- `brazilian-jiu-jitsu.html` → hub for all BJJ service-variant spokes
- `muay-thai.html` → hub for all Muay Thai spokes
- `mma.html` → hub for all MMA spokes
- `womens-jiu-jitsu.html` → hub for all Women's BJJ spokes
- `alavanca-hq.html` → hub for NE Calgary neighborhood spokes
- `alavanca-shawnessy.html` → hub for SW Calgary neighborhood spokes
- `alavanca-airdrie.html` → hub for Airdrie / north spokes
- `blog.html` → eventually hub for glossary (or spin up `/learn/index.html` as a dedicated hub)
- `memberships.html` → hub for pricing/offer spokes

**Linking rules for every new page:**
1. Each spoke links up to its service hub AND its location hub (two primary internal links above the fold).
2. Each spoke cross-links to 2–4 sibling spokes in its cluster (other neighborhoods for the same service; or other services in the same neighborhood).
3. Each spoke links into a related glossary or comparison page once, contextually.
4. Each spoke links to `free-class.html` via a primary CTA (also opens the lead modal).
5. Once a hub has 5+ spokes pointing in, add a "Locations near you" or "Programs for you" section to the hub that lists all spokes.

**Hub-page updates over time:**
- After Phase 1: `brazilian-jiu-jitsu.html` gets a new "BJJ by Neighborhood" section listing the 20 new location pages.
- After Phase 2: same hub adds a "BJJ by Audience" section with persona page links.
- Footer gets updated at Phase 4 to surface a "Nearby Areas" row (Cochrane, Okotoks, Chestermere, etc.).

## 6. Schema Strategy Per Page Type

| Page type | Required JSON-LD |
|---|---|
| Location page (`/locations/.../{service}.html`) | `LocalBusiness` (or `SportsActivityLocation`) with neighborhood name, pointing to parent Organization @id + `BreadcrumbList` + `Service` |
| Persona page (`/programs/{service}-for-{persona}.html`) | `Service` (with `audience` property set to the persona) + `BreadcrumbList` |
| Glossary page (`/learn/{term}.html`) | `Article` (if long-form) or `DefinedTerm` (if dictionary-style) + `BreadcrumbList` + `FAQPage` where applicable |
| Best-in-City page (`/guides/best-...`) | `ItemList` (listing the 3 Alavanca locations, plus contextual ranking criteria) + `BreadcrumbList` |
| Comparison page (`/compare/X-vs-Y.html`) | `Article` + `BreadcrumbList` + `FAQPage` (comparison Q&A) |
| Pricing page (`/memberships/...`) | `Offer` + `Product` / `Service` + `BreadcrumbList` |

All new pages inherit the shared Organization + WebSite schema from the homepage via `@id` references.

## 7. Content Uniqueness Rules (avoid thin-content penalty)

Every new page must include AT LEAST THREE of these unique elements beyond the template swap. This is the single biggest risk to programmatic SEO — Google penalizes template-farms, rewards pages with genuine differentiation.

Required per page type:

**Location pages MUST include:**
- 1 specific neighborhood landmark (park, mall, school, major intersection)
- Commute time from that neighborhood to the nearest Alavanca location (e.g., "7 minutes from Bridgeland to Alavanca HQ via Edmonton Trail")
- Parking / transit note specific to that neighborhood
- At least one sentence referencing a competitor martial arts gym in the neighborhood (by category, not by name — "the closest alternative BJJ academy is in a different quadrant, so most neighborhood locals train with us")
- A unique local testimonial quote if we have one from that neighborhood; otherwise, a rotating general testimonial with a note flagging the neighborhood

**Persona pages MUST include:**
- A persona-specific "what to expect" section (e.g., "What Adults Over 40 should know about BJJ" — joint considerations, training frequency, expected recovery time)
- A persona-specific first-class walkthrough
- A persona-specific FAQ (3–5 Q's)
- 2 persona-relevant testimonials (flag if we need to gather more)

**Glossary pages MUST include:**
- A first-paragraph answer block (featured-snippet-friendly)
- Alavanca coach quote or opinion on the topic (flag if we need to collect)
- Related terms section (internal linking)
- A "See this at Alavanca" CTA section tying the term back to a program

**Best-in-city pages MUST include:**
- Explicit ranking criteria ("We ranked based on: coaching depth, class variety, kids programs, facility quality, community")
- Honest acknowledgement of alternatives (one or two sentences on what other gyms do well — builds trust)
- All three Alavanca locations surfaced with distinct angles
- Last-updated date (refresh quarterly)

**Comparison pages MUST include:**
- A direct answer in the first paragraph (which is better for what goals)
- A side-by-side feature table
- 1–2 expert coach quotes
- Internal links to the relevant program pages

**Pricing pages MUST include:**
- Specific pricing tiers (or "starts at $X" where we have permission)
- What's included per tier
- A comparison to nearby competitor average pricing (research required)
- Financing / family discount / trial detail

## 9. Risks & Things the User Must Validate

**Local assumptions I made that require validation:**
1. **Neighborhood list** — I based the 30+ neighborhoods on common knowledge of Calgary and Airdrie. Before Phase 1 ships, validate which neighborhoods Alavanca already pulls members from (Google Business Profile reviews, member database, or just coach memory). Prioritize those.
2. **Nearby-town interest** — "Olds" appears in the testimonial ("I make the 2-hour round trip from Olds") so it's validated. Cochrane, Okotoks, and Chestermere are educated guesses — validate by checking if members drive in from those towns.
3. **Competitor landscape** — The roadmap assumes Alavanca has reasonable room to compete. If there's already a dominant Calgary BJJ site with 200+ indexed local pages, Phase 3 curation and comparisons become harder. Do a Google search for "bjj Calgary" and "muay thai Calgary" before Phase 3 to sanity-check.
4. **Pricing disclosure** — Phase 5 pricing pages require the user to commit to publishing actual pricing tiers. If they prefer to keep pricing private, convert those 8 pricing pages into "membership framework" pages instead (less competitive but still useful).

**SEO risks to monitor:**
- **Thin content** — if pages start slipping out of the index, the content uniqueness rules above aren't being met.
- **Cannibalization** — watch for the existing `brazilian-jiu-jitsu.html` page losing rankings to new location pages. Use `rel=canonical` correctly (self-referencing) and make sure location pages clearly target `{service} in {neighborhood}` intent, not head-term `{service}` intent.
- **Picsum vs real images** — every new page currently uses seeded placeholder images. Before deploy, swap to real photos where possible (especially for location pages — real shots of the neighborhood outside the gym, interior action shots).
