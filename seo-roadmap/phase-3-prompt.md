## Per-page content requirements

**Best-in-city pages must include:**
- Explicit "How we ranked" section (transparency over sales pitch)
- At least 3 candidate gyms listed (Alavanca + 2 legit alternatives — research the top Calgary BJJ/MT/MMA gyms before writing)
- Pros + cons per candidate (honest assessment, not smear)
- Why Alavanca — a specific differentiator section
- "Last updated" date (set to publish date; plan quarterly refreshes)
- Schema: `ItemList` + `BreadcrumbList`

**Comparison pages must include:**
- First-paragraph direct answer (featured-snippet bait — "which is better for X")
- Side-by-side feature table (history, technique, rules, fitness demands, learning curve)
- At least 2 coach quotes
- "Which should you start with?" decision tree or flowchart
- Internal links to the relevant Alavanca program pages
- Schema: `Article` + `FAQPage` + `BreadcrumbList`
