## Per-page content requirements

### Glossary / tutorial pages
- First-paragraph answer block (snippet-friendly, direct, 40–60 words)
- 500–800 words of coach-voice content — not thin
- At least one Alavanca coach quote (flag in post-build report if a real quote isn't available yet)
- Related terms grid (4 internal links to other glossary/program pages)
- "Try it at Alavanca" CTA section
- Schema: `Article` + `BreadcrumbList` + `FAQPage` where applicable

### Nearby-town location pages
- Commute time section (named highway + minutes)
- Honest disclaimer — "If you're {N} minutes out, we recommend committing to 2 classes/week minimum to make the drive worth it"
- Member-from-this-town anchor when available (Olds page → Darren McInes quote prominently)
- Single service anchor per town (usually BJJ) — don't cannibalize with multiple services per small town
- Schema: `Service` (with `areaServed` = town) + `BreadcrumbList`
