04 Why Alavanca

Champions are forged.
Not downloaded.

Professor Tim and the coaching staff built Alavanca around one idea: make real technique accessible without watering it down. White belts get the same fundamentals competitors drill. Kids get coaches who actually like working with kids. And parents get a room they feel good walking their family into.

Ten years in, our members are still on the mat. That's the number we're proudest of.

Three locations. One standard.

Same curriculum, same culture — whether you train in NE Calgary, Shawnessy, or Airdrie.

Beginner-friendly, lifer-deep.

Classes structured so the first-timer learns next to the purple belt, not instead of them.

Coaches who still train.

Every head coach is on the mat themselves every week. No retired instructors running scripts.

A room that holds itself together.

Clean facilities. Respectful training partners. A community you actually want to show up for.
