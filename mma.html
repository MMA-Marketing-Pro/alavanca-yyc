What MMA Actually Is

One system.
Every range.

Mixed Martial Arts is a full-contact combat sport that combines striking and grappling into one framework. It's the proving ground where techniques from Muay Thai, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, and wrestling have to actually work — or they get discarded.

At Alavanca, our MMA classes connect the dots between the disciplines we teach. You'll strike, you'll clinch, you'll take the fight to the ground and work back up. Whether you want to compete or just want the most honest combat-sports workout possible, this is where it lives.
