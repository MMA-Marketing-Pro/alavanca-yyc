July 8–12 · All Three Locations

Summer
Camps.

A week of martial arts mastery, themed adventures, and educational excursions — built for kids who want more than a screen this summer. Available at HQ, Shawnessy, and Airdrie.

Ask a Question
What the Week Looks Like

Unleash adventure
and learning.

From July 8th to 12th, Alavanca invites your child to an unforgettable week of fun, learning, and adventure. Camps run at all three locations — NE Calgary HQ, Shawnessy, and Airdrie — with small groups, experienced coaches, and a structured daily rhythm.

Martial arts mastery with expert coaches

Daily sessions introduce your child to martial arts. Discipline, focus, and self-defense in a safe, supportive environment.

Thematic exploration & educational excursions

Every week has its own theme paired with educational components — from nature to science. Learning doesn't stop during summer.

Safety-first environment with caring staff

Small group ratios, monitored supervision, instructors trained to keep kids safe while they grow.

Why Parents Book This Camp

Not just a babysit.
A real week.

Parents tell us the same thing every summer: they were looking for something that actually engaged their kid. Our campers leave the week with real self-defense fundamentals, new friends, and more confidence than when they arrived — plus they had a ton of fun doing it.

Drop-off and pickup times are structured around working parents' schedules. Spots are limited per location to keep group sizes right.
