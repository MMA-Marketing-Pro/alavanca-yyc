North of Calgary.

Welcome to Alavanca Airdrie — where we don't just teach martial arts, we build a lifestyle around them. Decide to Rise. Every person who walks through our doors has the potential to transcend their limits.

Situated at 2920 Kingsview Blvd SE #3153, our Airdrie location brings the same world-class instruction as our Calgary academies, close to home for families north of the city. Our expert trainers are masters of Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Muay Thai, and MMA.

Classes from early morning to late evening. Adult and children's programs. Clean facilities. A community that shows up for each other.
